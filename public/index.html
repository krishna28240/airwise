          04 · How it works

          Reliable rules, personalised by AI

          Health guidance must be dependable. AirWise uses a deterministic, unit-tested decision engine first, then uses generative AI only to explain and personalise.

          1. 1

            Collect

            Geocodes your city and fetches current AQI, PM2.5, PM10 and a 24-hour forecast from Open-Meteo (Copernicus CAMS).

          2. 2

            Assess

            Scores household vulnerability from age groups and conditions, and raises the risk level for sensitive families.

          3. 3

            Plan

            Finds the cleanest 2-hour window, builds a routine, gives each member a status and ranks commute options by CO₂ and exposure.

          4. 4

            Personalise

            Google Gemini writes a briefing in your language and answers follow-up questions, grounded in the plan.

          Secure by design

          Strict CSP, input allow-lists, rate limiting and a server-side API key.

          SQLite persistence

          Plans are stored in an embedded database with a REST API for history and statistics.

          Insights dashboard

          Trend chart, category breakdown and CSV export to track your exposure.

          Accessible & multilingual

          Keyboard and screen-reader friendly, responsive, with six briefing languages.
